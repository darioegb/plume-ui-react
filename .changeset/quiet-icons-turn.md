`FileUpload` shows an upload icon in its default dropzone, and the new `FileUploadIcon` part lets you replace it. `Carousel` gains `orientation="vertical"` and `controls="inline"`, and `CarouselDots` gains `appearance` (`dots`, `bars`, `numbers` and `thumbnails`), so the indicators and the layout can change, not only the buttons.
