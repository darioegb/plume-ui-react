Adds `Dropdown` (with `DropdownTrigger`, `DropdownMenu`, `DropdownItem`, `DropdownSeparator` and `DropdownLabel`): a menu of actions that follows the WAI-ARIA menu button pattern. The trigger opens it with a click, <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd> or the arrows; the arrows, <kbd>Home</kbd>, <kbd>End</kbd> and typing a few letters move between the items, <kbd>Esc</kbd> and <kbd>Tab</kbd> close it and give the focus back to the trigger, and a disabled item is skipped. Items can have an icon and a shortcut, be links (`href`) and keep the menu open (`closeOnSelect`). It has `elevated`, `outline` and `unstyled` variants and `sm`, `md` and `lg` sizes, opens on the side given by `placement` and flips or shifts to stay on the screen.
