Adds `Avatar` (with `AvatarGroup`), `Alert` (with `AlertTitle` and `AlertDescription`) and `Drawer` (with `DrawerTrigger`, `DrawerContent`, `DrawerHeader`, `DrawerBody`, `DrawerFooter` and `DrawerClose`). `Avatar` shows an image, the initials of a name or an icon, a presence status and a stacked group with a counter. `Alert` is the inline message with `info`, `success`, `warning` and `error` statuses, an action and a dismiss button. `Drawer` is a `Modal` that slides in from the left, the right, the top or the bottom, with rounded or square corners. `Spinner` accepts an `aria-label`.
