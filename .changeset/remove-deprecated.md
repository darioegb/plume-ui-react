Removes what was left as an alias or deprecated before 1.0. The loose class props of the parts are gone in favor of `classNames`: `inputClassName`, `labelClassName`, `revealClassName`, `countClassName`, `panelClassName`, `backdropClassName`, `bodyClassName`, `listClassName`, `separatorClassName`, `toastClassName`, `lineClassName`, `indicatorClassName`, `valueClassName`, `dotClassName`, `containerClassName`, `loadingClassName`, `sortClassName` and `emptyStateClassName`. The `useClickOutside` hook is removed. The docs have an "Upgrading to 1.0" guide with the equivalences, instead of the migration guide from the `@plume-ui-react/*` packages.
