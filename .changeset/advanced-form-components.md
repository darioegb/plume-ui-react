Adds `NumberInput`: a field for a number, with buttons to subtract and add and the keyboard of a spin button (<kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> add `step`, <kbd>PageUp</kbd> and <kbd>PageDown</kbd> a `largeStep`, <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> go to `min` and `max`). It is a text input with `role="spinbutton"` rather than `type="number"`, so the mouse wheel does not change it and the text can be a partial number while the user types; the value is clamped to `min`/`max` and rounded to `precision` when the field loses focus or on <kbd>Enter</kbd>, and it is `null` when the field is empty. `format` and `parse` show and read a currency, a unit or a locale, and the formatted text is exposed as `aria-valuetext`. The buttons keep adding while they are held, and with a `name` the plain number is submitted in a hidden input. It has the `outline`, `filled`, `underline` and `unstyled` variants of `Input`, composes with `Field`, and `classNames` styles its `input`, `decrement` and `increment`.

Adds `InputPin`: a row of one-character boxes for a one-time code or a PIN. Typing moves to the next box, <kbd>Backspace</kbd> goes back, the arrows, <kbd>Home</kbd> and <kbd>End</kbd> move between them, and a pasted code (or one that arrives whole from the autofill of a phone) is spread over the boxes. `type` limits the characters to digits, letters and digits, or anything but spaces, `mask` hides them, `onComplete` runs with the last box and `focusOnMount` focuses the first. It is a `<fieldset>` with one named input per character (`Digit 1 of 6`) and a single tab stop on the next box to fill; with a `name` the code is submitted in a hidden input, and it composes with `Field`.

Adds `Rating`: a row of stars, or of any icon, to give or show a score. It is a group of radio buttons, so the arrows change the score, and `allowHalf` gives every star two radios for half scores. The stars follow the pointer as a preview, `clearable` lets the user remove the score, and `icon`, `emptyIcon`, `classNames` and `getItemLabel` change how it looks and is announced. A `readOnly` rating is text (`3 out of 5`) with the icons hidden, not a group of disabled radios. It composes with `Field` and takes the `solid` and `unstyled` variants.

`Input` gets `revealable`: with `type="password"` it adds a button, in the place of `elementAfter`, that shows or hides the password (a toggle button with `aria-pressed`, named by `revealLabel`, and styled in `unstyled` with `revealClassName`). It also accepts `autoComplete`, and the group of an `Input` with addons or elements keeps its row in the `unstyled` variant.
