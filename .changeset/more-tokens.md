New design tokens, all customizable in the theme and available as CSS variables and as Tailwind utilities: motion (`duration-fast/normal/slow` and `easing-standard/out/in-out`), `opacity-disabled`, the focus ring (`width`, `offset` and `color`), `border-width-thin/medium/thick`, `control-height-sm/md/lg`, the semantic color `backdrop`, the half steps `0.5`, `1.5` and `2.5` of the spacing scale (`--plume-space-0-5`), the font sizes `3xl` to `5xl`, `letter-spacing`, `font-family-mono`, dark shadows of their own, the shadows `raised` and `panel`, and text styles (`heading-1` to `heading-6`, `body`, `body-sm`, `label` and `caption`).
